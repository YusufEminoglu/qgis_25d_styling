about=QGIS 2.5D Styling is a specialized studio dock for transforming flat 2D building footprint polygons into publication-grade 2.5D architectural massing models directly in QGIS. Features an integrated OSM Overpass downloader to fetch building footprints and level/height tags for any extent, dynamic solar illumination with aspect-based facade shading, multi-layer floor divisions (mullions), directional ground shadows, and 8 curated architectural presets (Swiss Minimalist, Dark Obsidian, Warm Terracotta, Vintage Blueprint, Emerald Glass, Cyberpunk Neon, Solar Heatmap, and Monochrome Print). Includes headless Processing algorithms and standalone QML style export. Repository: https://github.com/YusufEminoglu/qgis_25d_styling
